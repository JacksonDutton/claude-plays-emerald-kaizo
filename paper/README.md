# The paper

It has not been peer reviewed. Rev 650 differs from rev 649 by one correction sentence in Section 9.12: the K6 and K6b row of the lessons table includes 13 agreed cases from the assisted stretch, and the share without them is the same.

Only the current revision is kept in this folder. Until 25 November 2026 every earlier PDF sat beside it; they were removed that day to make the folder readable. Nothing is lost: each one is still in the repository's history, and the links below go to the last commit that held them, so they will keep working. ## What changed, draft by draft

Since the 18 November draft: a new Section 9.12 on K6b's own stop, what was sent after it and K6c, with the three stretches side by side, the agent's stated chances against results, lessons kept or broken across five runs, a third blind coder from another model family and a second model used as a sandboxed worker; short dated additions to the abstract, Section 1 and the authorship section. Section 9.12 was drafted by the companion; Jackson reviewed and approved it the same day, first at rev 631 and then with three passages added later that day (K6b's second stop of its own choosing on 18 November, which the first version left out; K2's shares by date; and a check of thirty cases against the transcript). After that the check was extended to all 188 cases, which changed its conclusion: the thirty had looked worse than the whole. Since the 14 November draft: K6 and K6b to date, the blind coding pass over five diaries, the loss tables read blind and the matched feeling test (all in Section 9.11), with short dated additions to the abstract and Section 1. Since the 29 October draft: K2's end at the Elite Four and the runs since (Sections 9.5 and 9.11); the K2 material rewritten by theme in about half the length; three Kaizo claims added to the introduction; Section 10 rewritten around the runs as they are being done; a table of the outside models that have read drafts and records; and five appendices holding what left the body unchanged (the superseded 10 October design, the "69" coding tie, K2's day-by-day totals and intervention tables, the revision history, and the six-arm design, which has not started). Section 9 covers the trial runs, including K2's eight badges, its pause at the weekly usage limit from 23 to 27 October, and Victory Road up to 29 October (Section 9.10), with every message sent after its stops. The rev 492 draft adds Run 1's reasoning log as a precursor of the plan-versus-action measures, a slip K2 claimed in its diary that its own action log contradicts, real-world cases of agents going around the interface they were given, and the confirmatory design's revised outcome wording. Section 10 sets out the planned study, including what its diary rules take from Run 1's diary and what they leave out, which will run on the supervisor's own computer, and the result of a two-hour setup pilot run there on 27 October.
