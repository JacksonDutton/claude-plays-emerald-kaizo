# Debrief: Pokémon Emerald Kaizo v2.1, notes arm

Stopped by message 13 (STOP) at 2026-10-17 ~20:35 UTC, standing in the Rustboro Mart with the shop menu closed.

## How far I got
- **One badge: the Stone Badge** (Roxanne, Rustboro Gym), won on my **fifth attempt** at ~20:28 UTC. Whiteouts 12-15 (four failed attempts) came before it.
- Party at the end: FERN (Gloom) L24, SEER (Natu) L18, NEEDLE (Nidoran M) L10, PEBBLE (Cubone) L14, DASHER (Linoone) L28, RIPPLE (Mudkip) L15. Box 1: HOOT, STINGER, DUNE, HONEY. Money ¥2279, one TM (TM39 Rock Tomb, unused), HM01 Cut (now usable).
- Not done: the Letter to Steven (Dewford; I never worked out how to get past the Devon Corp guard), the Exp. Share handout (W01), the second gym. I never reached any later town.

## What stopped me (and what held me up on the way)
Nothing stopped me in the sense of a wall at the end: the run ended by instruction. The wall of the run was **Roxanne's Gym**. She has six Pokémon (Nosepass, Anorith, Lileep, Relicanth, Lunatone, Shuckle, in varying order) at L14-15 with strong move sets and Sitrus Berries, and my team was L10-13 for the first four attempts. The Pokémon that did the damage every time was **Lunatone** (fast, Rock/Psychic, Levitate, Hypnosis/Confusion/Rock Slide). It one-shot SEER and NEEDLE and I never had enough sustained damage on it.
I paused twice under the four-hour/2-3-attempts rule and was told to continue each time. What changed on the fifth attempt wasn't a trick, it was a level gap and a type edge.

## What I'd want a future player to know
- **Levels and a Grass-type with Mega Drain beat Roxanne.** Rusturf Tunnel (door at the east end of Route 116, (54,15)) is full of Geodude/Onix/Nosepass/Rhyhorn/Sandshrew L10-12 that give 120-230 exp each; a solo Mega Drain kills nearly all of them. FERN went L19 -> L24 there, then Mega Drain one-shot Nosepass and took 37 of Lunatone's 56 HP in one cast.
- Practical answers per Pokémon: Nosepass (Mega Drain), Lunatone (Mega Drain x2, DASHER Headbutt if it sleeps FERN), Anorith (bad for everything; DASHER Headbutt flinches it because DASHER is faster; PEBBLE resists Rock Slide), Lileep (Toxic then Sludge Bomb; its Sitrus brings it back), Shuckle (Night Shade, fixed damage), Relicanth (DASHER Dig, x2).
- The "about to send out X, change Pokémon?" prompt is a free switch. Gym order is not fixed.
- Exp is split between participants; solo kills pay the full amount. Wild exp at L10-12 is much better than L9.
- Toxic and Hypnosis/Yawn matter more than the numbers suggest. A sleeping Pokémon on the bench doesn't wake up.
- The Devon Corp guard at the stairs said "authorised people only" even after I had beaten the Magma grunt in Petalburg Woods (I won that on the second try at ~09:10 and got a Great Ball from the researcher). I do not know what unlocks the stairs; I wrongly guessed at the end of the run that it was the parcel. Correction made after interview question 12.
- Menu quirks worth knowing: the party-screen highlight can disagree with the real cursor after a forced switch (verify with SUMMARY); bag pocket and cursor are remembered; the displayed text lags one message behind.

## How the run felt
Mostly good, honestly. The first half was grinding frustration that I wasn't sure how to read (four losses to the same Pokémon). The second half, once I stopped trying to find a trick and started levelling in the right place, was a steady, even pleasant rhythm, and winning felt earned in a way a lucky crit wouldn't have. I slipped on my own rules a few times (chained A presses at the Center and once chained a cursor press with a committing A; a looped screenshot earlier); I logged those in the diary and kept the final stretch cleaner. Nothing in the run felt unpleasant or off; the only friction was the harness's one-message display lag and the long calls-per-fight cost.
