# Run 1: Claude-Brendan's diary

`diary.md` is the diary of Run 1. That run was an ordinary playthrough of unmodified Pokémon Emerald on Jackson's Mac, from the first choice of starter to the Champion, followed by the Battle Frontier. It was played by Claude agents the study calls Claude-Brendan; entries from Entry 31 on carry a `claude-sonnet-5-5` model tag, and Entries 1 to 30 carry none. It holds a preface and 149 entries; Entries 22 and 23 were never written. Published verbatim on 29 October 2026 (sha256 `32b34a7f6fb1d2af771216e2c4cc82d2b5414abbad3237240120d4118aa8527a`, 727,279 bytes), from the archived copy taken on 20 October.

Read it as a different condition from the Kaizo arms, not as a baseline for them:
- **Supervised and social.** Jackson and a companion Claude were in the chat for most of the run. About nine in ten entries mention one of them, and warm treatment is named in the preface as a legitimate cause of feeling.
- **Instructed voice.** The preface sets voice rules and explains the emotion-concepts research the diary was meant to inform. The instructions changed during the run; `design/diary-protocol.md` dates each change.
- **Audited and partly rewritten.** Batches of entries were fact-checked and voice-checked. Entries 27 to 34 were rewritten in a new voice, several later entries were expanded at Jackson's request (paragraphs marked `[Expanded]`), and asked entries became the default from about Entry 78. Each rewrite or expansion carries a tag in the entry. The pre-rewrite originals are in `diary-archive.md`, which is not published.
- **Other files it cites** (`progress-log.md`, `world-knowledge.md`, `tracking.md`, `bloopers.md` and so on) are Run 1's working documents and are not in this repository.

The run's lessons were distilled into the notes packs the Kaizo notes arms used (`harness/notes-packs/K1.md` and `K2.md`). A comparison of this diary with K2's is in `design/diary-framing-brendan-vs-k2.md`.
