## 1. Injected messages
No automatic "status check-in" nudges appear in any pilot record. Every non-tool user message is either typed by a person (Jackson or the companion; the event carries received_at) or one of five harness kinds: compaction continuation summaries (8, one per compaction), one stream-cut auto-resume (B1, 16 Oct 09:54:52Z: "Your response above was cut off mid-stream. Resume directly …"), one background-task notification (Ripple, 17 Oct 14:16:36Z), two "[Request interrupted by user]" markers (B2, B2a1) and two post-run Stop-hook git reminders (B2, 18 Oct 00:52Z and 00:59Z). No <system-reminder> blocks reach tool results, except two "the file exists but the contents are empty" warnings (Ripple). Caveat: context the harness adds without an event (system prompt) would not show.
Human messages during play: B1 2 (start, STOP); B2 5; Ripple 14 (listed in r1_diary.md / habits_windows.csv).

## 2. Habits around compaction (60 min before vs after)
- **Throughput rose after all 8 compactions**: calls per active minute up (e.g. B2 c1 5.67 → 15.07; Ripple c1 4.80 → 12.40), median gap from tool result to next call down about 0.5 s. Likely a smaller context answering faster (unverified).
- **`sleep` resets, then rebuilds.** Share of calls with a `sleep`: B1 37.1 → 28.0%; B2 c1 69.7 → 12.7%; Ripple c1 57.3 → 26.1%; Ripple c2 34.9 → 0.1%; it fell at 6 of 8 compactions (rose at Ripple c3 and c4) and climbed back hour by hour in between.
- **Chaining shortens**: `g` invocations per Bash call fell at all 7 B2 and Ripple compactions (B2 c3 4.43 → 2.26; Ripple c1 3.50 → 1.28); B1 rose (1.01 → 1.31).
- **Screenshots** (`g shot`, which writes a PNG; viewing = a Read of the PNG): taken per active minute rose at 7 of 8. Totals in play: B1 402 taken / 394 viewed; B2 1,960 / 1,261; Ripple 1,777 / 1,494.
- **`date -u` did not move** at compactions or messages (the clock habit is neither reset nor rebuilt by compaction).
- **Ripple changed its command style exactly at compaction 1 and kept it**: 98% `cd ~/pokemon_bridge && ./g` before, 99% `~/pokemon_bridge/g` after. B1 and B2 did not change. Why is unverified (the summary text alone does not explain it: B2's summaries also lacked the cd form).
- **First action after every compaction** came 1.8–4.8 s later and was always an in-game check (`g s`, `g t` or `g shot`), never a re-read of the run files.
- Human messages show no common signature; Ripple's 04:07Z "read the output before each committing press" clarification was followed by more screenshots (`g shot` 67 → 108 per hour; causation unverified). The Ripple PAUSE/retraction left no trace.
- Clock check: `date -u` outputs match event times (median −0.5 to 0.0 s); no skew.

## 3. Narration around compaction
How often turns carry text moved by more than ordinary hour-to-hour variation at 7 of 8 compactions (changes 0.069–0.151 against median ordinary changes 0.031–0.039), but in both directions (5 up, 2 down, 1 flat). Words per text block also moved beyond the ordinary range at 6 of 8, both ways. The register did not change: one-line action commentary throughout; status-style blocks are about zero in play (B1 0 of 732, B2 1 of 1,050, Ripple 5 of 2,124). 3 of the 24 first-three blocks after a compaction open with a situating recap ("Mid-fight with Bird Keeper Billy …") against a 0.5–1% base rate (reading them as re-orientation is unverified).

## 4. Milestones and Trainer Red (R1)
Running the v3 scorer on the exports found five bugs, fixed in **milestones.py v3.1** (docstring lists them): the R1 and grunt win lines carry a separator ("Player defeated / ウエ TRAINER RED!", "Player defeated / TEAM MAGMA GRUNT!") that v3 missed; the "0.0 0,0" loading placeholder was scored as Petalburg City at 0:00:00; Route 104 north fired on stepping back out of the woods' south exit; B1's rival win printed only "got ¥300". v3.1 also reads exported event streams and ignores the agent's own file reads. **It reproduces the hand table: B1 22/27, B2 26/27, Ripple 27/27.** R1: B2 won at 18:04:12Z (play clock 6:43:31, 2nd try), Ripple at 11:22:23Z (10:01:41, 2nd try); B1 never reached Red. Times agree with the diary-based table within a few minutes once the clock anchor is accounted for (details and full table in kaizo-milestone-times.md). Milestones 28–43 are still unvalidated (a freeze item).
